A curated list of receptions, parties, happy hours, and networking socials happening during SC26 week — official conference receptions, vendor parties, and community events, with registration links.

Co-author of **CoCoDiff** (SC'26) and **Difflet/DiffForge**, an open-source diffusion inference engine for AWS Trainium ([github.com/ai-decentralized/difflet](https://github.com/ai-decentralized/difflet)). ## 🔥 Highlights for AI accelerator & cloud folks

- **Cerebras** — only at the VAST Data nightcap (Mon 20:00, Fatpour, next to the venue).
- **AMD** — the HPE Industry Reception at Adler Planetarium (Tue 19:00) is the AMD-branded social; no RSVP, badge only.
- **Google Cloud** — GCACC rooftop reception Mon 17:00 (register via the GCACC page). Google also mentions a joint reception with NVIDIA, but no public date/venue.
- **NVIDIA** — no standalone reception; find them at Petaflop (Tue), the MinIO Tiki Bar (Wed 14:00), and as co-sponsor of Oracle's (closed) reception.
- **AWS** — only the booth-level "Quantummania" reception (Wed 16:00, booth #4819), quantum-themed rather than Trainium-themed.
- **No SC26 reception found** (checked Nov 11): Intel, Groq, SambaNova, Tenstorrent, d-Matrix, Etched, Microsoft Azure, CoreWeave, Lambda, Dell, Supermicro.

## ⚠️ Scheduling collisions worth knowing

- **Monday night:** the two 20:00–22:00 events (Spybar vs. Fatpour) fully overlap; the Grand Opening Gala (19:00–21:00) and the Google Cloud reception (17:00–19:00) bracket them. Beowulf Bash runs until midnight if you want a late option.
- **Tuesday night is the crunch:** Posters Reception, Lenovo, Petaflop, HPE/AMD, Arista, and Netris all overlap — realistically pick two.
- **Wednesday:** the AWS booth reception (16:00–18:00) overlaps the Students & Early Career Reception in the same building; Everpure (18:30) chains nicely right after it.
